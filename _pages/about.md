I am a PhD candidate in the [Department of Political Science](https://polisci.duke.edu/) at Duke University, specializing in Security, Peace, and Conflict. I study topics at the intersection of International Relations and Comparative Politics with a regional focus on the Middle East. My research explores the causes and consequences of foreign policy, forced migration, and authoritarian nostalgia in the region. I am also passionate about teaching. At Duke, I have served as a teaching assistant for courses in International Relations and U.S. Foreign Policy and completed Duke’s [Certificate in College Teaching](https://gradschool.duke.edu/professional-development/programs/certificate-college-teaching/). I hold an MS in Political Science from the University of Utah and a BA in Middle East Studies and Arabic from the University of Arkansas.
